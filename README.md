# Caleb Williams — 3rd & Long Analysis: 2024 vs 2025
**Chicago Bears | NFL Play-by-Play Analysis**

## Overview
An investigation into Caleb Williams' 3rd and long performance across his 
first two NFL seasons. The 2024 analysis identifies a significant sack rate 
vulnerability, investigates its causes, and rules out common explanations. 
The 2025 notebook measures how dramatically the situation improved in year two.

## Key Finding
Caleb Williams' sack rate on 3rd and long dropped from 30.9% in 2024 — 
nearly 4x the NFL average — to 6.7% in 2025, below the league average. 
Completion percentage jumped 20 points and EPA flipped from negative to 
positive over the same period.

## Notebooks
- `caleb_williams_3rd_down_2024.ipynb` — Identifying and investigating 
the 2024 3rd and long vulnerability
- `caleb_williams_3rd_down_2025.ipynb` — Year-over-year comparison and 
conclusion
